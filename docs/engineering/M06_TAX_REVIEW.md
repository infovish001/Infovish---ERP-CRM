# M06 Sri Lanka invoice and tax review

Primary sources rechecked 2026-10-31 at the user's continuing request:

- [Gazette 2481/22](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Gazette_Documents/2026_2481-22_E.pdf), read all four pages, defines tax-invoice particulars, TINs, serial/date/currency presentation and taxable-supply-only content. [Gazette 2500/106](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Gazette_Documents/2026_2500_106_E.pdf) moves its effect to October 2026.
- [VAT Act consolidation](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Value%20Added%20Tax_Acts/VAT_Act_No_14%5BE%5D_2002_%28Consolidation_2025%29.pdf) and [IRD original Act section 20](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Value%20Added%20Tax_Acts/VATActNo14%5BE%5D2002.pdf) address registered purchasers' written requests, issue timing, retained duplicates and marked replacement copies. M06 narrowly requires confirmed request evidence and a supply no more than 28 days earlier; it does not determine legal time of supply or validate actual certificates.
- [2026 amendment](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Value%20Added%20Tax_Acts/VAT_Act_No_14-2026_E.PDF) changes financial-services VAT separately. M05's sourced ordinary VAT18 pricing is preserved; financial VAT, SSCL liabilities, imports, withholding and filings remain excluded. Bills' recoverable VAT is an explicit operator declaration/evidence, not automated entitlement to credit.

All-standard domestic Sales orders only can become tax invoices; actual seller/purchaser particulars, issue/supply dates, unique continuous number and original/duplicate/copy issuance are retained. Mixed and special regimes are blocked. Other zero/exempt/unregistered orders produce commercial non-tax invoices only, without positive VAT. No statutory correction/credit-note workflow is inferred from accounting reversals. A qualified business reviewer must confirm applicability, registration, recognition, retention and exception handling before release; implementation/source review is not legal certification.
