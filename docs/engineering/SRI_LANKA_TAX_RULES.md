# Sri Lanka Sales tax baseline

Reviewed 2026-10-30 from primary IRD publications at the user's request. Implementation scope is ordinary LKR quotations/orders, not tax invoices or returns. No company registration or supply classification is inferred.

- Ordinary standard VAT is 18% from 2024-01-01; zero rating and exemption are separate treatments. Section 2 and the valuation/registration/invoice provisions are in the [IRD consolidated VAT Act through 2025](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Value%20Added%20Tax_Acts/VAT_Act_No_14%5BE%5D_2002_(Consolidation_2025).pdf). The [IRD VAT page](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/Type%20of%20Taxes/SitePages/Value%20Added%20Tax%20(VAT).aspx?menuid=1204) corroborates the standard rate. Operators must declare actual seller registration and legal basis for zero/exempt supplies. Ordinary negotiated consideration only; special valuation cases require later policy.
- The [2026 amendment, Act 14](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Value%20Added%20Tax_Acts/VAT_Act_No_14-2026_E.PDF) changes financial-services VAT under section 25C to 20.5% from July 2026. That is a different regime, excluded from this calculator; do not apply ordinary VAT18 to financial-services value addition.
- Tax-invoice format requirements take effect October 2026 under [Gazette 2500/106](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Gazette_Documents/2026_2500_106_E.pdf), amending Gazette 2481/22. M05 PDFs are explicitly quotation/order documents, not evidence of invoice compliance. Invoice generation belongs to Finance.
- [IRD SSCL guidance](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/Type%20of%20Taxes/SitePages/Social%20Security%20Contribution%20Levy%20(SSCL).aspx) describes a 2.5% levy on activity-dependent liable turnover. A flat extra customer VAT-style charge would not implement that liability correctly. SSCL computation/filing, customs, withholding and sector-specific levies require separate policies and are excluded.

Engineering rounding: six-place quantity/unit-price/discount inputs, exact decimal arithmetic, net lines half-up to cents, document VAT on the summed standard-rated base half-up to cents. This chosen quotation policy is not presented as a mandatory statutory rounding method. Persist the rule version `LK-VAT-2026-10-30`, selected classifications, registration/profile revision and totals on each document. No automatic legislative updates or exemption classifier. An owner must review later legislative changes and business applicability before commercial use.
