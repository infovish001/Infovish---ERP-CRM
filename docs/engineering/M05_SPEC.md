# M05 Sales specification

Authorized by the user's continuation after verified M04 on 2026-10-30. Stop before M06. PRD SAL is a grouped range; the labels below refine acceptance without inventing individual PRD requirements. Related requirements: E2E-004/005, NFR-001/002/003/006/007/009/013, DEP-001/002/004/005/007 and SAD sections 3-6/9/10/12.

1. Sales is a bundled schema-4 module depending on Parties, Catalog and CRM public contracts. Privileged MFA users create/edit/issue/cancel/convert quotations. Granted-company members/auditors may read company Sales documents; Sales does not inherit CRM assignment privacy. Opportunity links still require the CRM contract's assigned-record authorization. No cross-module private queries.
2. Explicit company tax profile is required: Sri Lanka, LKR, seller legal name/address, VAT registration status and identifier/effective date where registered. Registration is an operator declaration, never inferred from a company name/turnover. Profile changes have revisions/audit; old documents retain snapshots. User requested internet-sourced Sri Lankan tax rules; see SRI_LANKA_TAX_RULES.md.
3. Draft quotes have a permanent QTN reference, active customer, optional authorized open opportunity for that customer, issue date, validity date, terms and 1-50 product/service variant lines. All prices are explicit tax-exclusive decimal strings; no silent list-price substitution. Snapshot customer, seller, SKU/name/unit/currency and classification. No custom HTML/assets or uploads.
4. Quantities have six decimal places and must be positive. Unit prices allow 14 integer/six fractional digits; discounts 0-100 percent up to six decimals. Compute exact quantity*price*(1-discount/100), round each net line half-up to two decimals, sum net by tax treatment, calculate 18% VAT on the standard-rated document subtotal once, round half-up to cents, then add. Server owns totals; numeric overflow is rejected. This is an explicit quotation policy, not a claim of a legislated line-rounding method. Preserve six-place inputs and two-place LKR totals.
5. Tax codes distinguish standard VAT18, zero-rated, exempt and unregistered. Registered suppliers cannot select unregistered; unregistered suppliers cannot charge VAT. Zero/exempt lines require an explicit legal basis, not automated classification. Standard-rated goods/services only; financial-services VAT, imports/customs, VAT-inclusive prices, withholding, SSCL liabilities, deemed/special valuation and returns are outside this Sales calculator. Reject unsupported profiles/currencies. Effective-date validation prevents pre-registration VAT.
6. Draft edits require current revision and revalidate snapshots/prices/taxes. Issue freezes content and records an internal issue action (no email/delivery). Issued documents can be cancelled with a reason or converted before expiry into exactly one immutable confirmed SO order. Conversion does not recalculate historical prices or modify CRM. Active customer/products and current tax-profile revision are required when issuing/converting; changed policy requires cancelling and creating a new quote. Permanent quote/order link, atomic counters/lines/history/audit/outbox/idempotency and unique quote constraint survive matching retries/concurrency. Conflicting/stale transitions return 409. Cancelled/converted/confirmed documents cannot be edited/deleted or reopened.
7. Authorized detail/list/search/filter/pagination (25), bounded latest-100 immutable history, authenticated downloadable quotation/order PDF from snapshots. PDF labels document type/status and 'Not a tax invoice'; draft watermark; no external resource/PHP/JavaScript access. No invoice, payment, shipment, stock reservation, recognized revenue or accounting posting is created. Public immutable Orders/OrderData contract enables future consumers.
8. Real PostgreSQL tests verify totals/rounding/range/tax policy, snapshot immutability, lifecycle, retry/concurrency, rollback, company/tenant/role/MFA/module boundaries, PDF escaping/auth, schema upgrade preservation and ID-only worker receipts. UI interaction tests, type/lint/build, fresh-cache Pint/static/boundary/OpenAPI/audits/secrets, host/container suites, browser acceptance and hosted CI are required. Actual workspace upgrades preserve data and leave Sales inactive; all browser records use a disposable tenant.

M05 functional acceptance is distinct from R1 commercial release. Produce an explicit release-gate ledger; do not mark Released or deploy publicly without approved operational ownership, reviewed applicability/localization, measured workload/security and recovery evidence. Complete all verifiable local gates and document external prerequisites. CPQ, commissions, contracts, advanced price lists, fulfillment, returns and Finance remain deferred. M06 requires new authorization.
